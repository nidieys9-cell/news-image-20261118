# The World in One Day — Wednesday, 18 November 2026

An original infographic summing up the world news of **Wednesday, 18 November 2026**
in one 1000 × 1000 PNG image.

Competition: https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261118

## How it is built

- `draw.py` — original Python (Pillow) drawing script, written for this entry.
  Run `python3 draw.py` to regenerate `entry.png`.
- `entry.png` — the generated infographic (1000 × 1000, under 1 MiB).
- `main.ts` — make.fun starter program (Node 22, standard library only);
  it copies `entry.png` to the output directory:
  `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`

## The six stories and their sources

All stories happened, or were first reported, on 18 November 2026 and were
verified from that day's reporting before drawing:

1. **Diplomacy — Xi meets Putin in Shenzhen.** Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin
   met in Shenzhen on the sidelines of APEC, ahead of planned trilateral talks
   with Donald Trump. — AFP / Associated Press, via Open Chronicle Global News
   Brief, 18 Nov 2026, and Horizon daily brief, 18 Nov 2026.
2. **Ukraine war — Kerch Bridge hit.** Ukrainian drones and missiles struck
   the bridge linking Russia to annexed Crimea and part of a road span
   collapsed into the strait. — Horizon daily brief, 18 Nov 2026.
3. **Gulf escalation — Iran drones target Kuwait and Bahrain.** Iran said it
   sent drones at US installations in Kuwait, Bahrain said it intercepted
   attacks, and US officials said US aircraft struck a missile launch site on
   Qeshm Island. — Horizon daily brief and Open Chronicle, 18 Nov 2026.
4. **US politics — Peltola wins Alaska.** Democrat Mary Peltola defeated
   Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan in Alaska, giving Democrats a projected
   56–44 majority in the incoming US Senate. — Associated Press / Alaska
   Public Media, via Open Chronicle, 18 Nov 2026.
5. **Health — US loses measles-free status.** The United States lost its
   measles elimination status after 26 years, with more than 5,400 cases and
   10 deaths reported. — Horizon daily brief, 18 Nov 2026.
6. **Humanitarian — Yemen children.** UNICEF said at least 64 children had
   been killed and 64 injured in Yemen since 3 September amid renewed
   hostilities, with an estimated 330,000 people displaced. — UN News Today,
   18 Nov 2026.
